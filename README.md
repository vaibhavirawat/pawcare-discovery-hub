# PawCare Discovery Hub

Create a modern, mobile-responsive homepage and search discovery prototype for 'PawCare' (Practo for Animals)—an animal-care discovery platform. Use a warm, trustworthy palette (emerald green, soft cream, slate grey). Include: 1. Sticky Navbar: Logo, 'Emergency 24/7 Ambulance' red button, 'List Your Business' outline button, and 'Submit a Correction' link. 2. Hero Section: Headline 'Trusted Care for Every Paw & Claw', location auto-detect dropdown, search bar ('Search vets, clinics, pet boarding, NGOs...'), and quick-category pills (Veterinary Clinics, 24/7 Hospitals, Pet Boarding, Diagnostics, Ambulances, Animal NGOs). 3. Interactive Search & Filter Section: Side/top filter bar with toggles for 'Open Now / 24x7', 'Emergency Ambulance', 'Animal Type (Dogs, Cats, Birds, Exotic, Street/Stray)', 'Verified Provider', and 'Distance'. 4. Provider Listing Cards (show 6 realistic cards): Include Provider Name, Verified Badge, Category badge, Star rating + review count, Distance, Timings, Services tags, Fees, and two buttons: 'Book Appointment / Call Now' and 'Get Directions'. 5. Bottom Community Banner: 'Spot outdated info or know a local animal NGO? [Submit a Correction] or [List Your Facility for Free]'. Make the filters interactive so clicking categories or toggles filters the cards.
